FAQ

Common questions from Christian charities

What does a Christian digital marketing agency actually do differently?

We work exclusively with Christian charities, focused on education, development and creating real value within the charity structure. Practically, that means three methods working together: natural keyword SEO, very specific targeted advertising, and Google Ad Grant management.

What is the Google Ad Grant, and can any Christian charity use it?

It gives eligible registered charities up to $10,000 (around £8,000) of free monthly Google Ads spend. Most UK-registered charities qualify, though the account needs managing to Google's compliance rules or the grant can be suspended.

How much does it cost to work with a Christian digital marketing agency?

We work with Christian charities at a reduced rate of £36 per hour. Our continuing commercial work helps subsidise that rate, and ad spend is often covered in full or in part by the Google Ad Grant.

What kind of charities benefit most from this approach?

It's particularly valuable for charities with products or tools they can sell or transact, and for events that need exposure — a course, a resource, a physical product or a conference.
