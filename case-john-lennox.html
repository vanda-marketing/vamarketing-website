Protecting John Lennox's work online

Making sure people searching for John Lennox find the real John Lennox, and not imitations, in an age of AI.

Google Ad Grants SEO Brand protection
Asif Valiji20 October 20265 min read

Meeting John

We met John Lennox once, at an event for The Oxford Centre for Christian Apologetics (OCCA). Meeting him in person, we learnt very quickly that this is a man who loves God and is on a mission to share the Word of God and the gospel of Jesus Christ with as many people as he can. In particular, he is committed to breaking through misconceptions and dealing with the heart of the matter: the love of Jesus Christ, the good news, and the prospect of a relationship with God that is like nothing else.

Working through our operational partner

Through our work with OCCA, where John Lennox is president, we worked closely over time with its operations team on the development and improvement of John's own website. That included the site's data structure and categorisation, which was carried out by OCCA's operations manager and director, with our support where it was needed. You can read more about that relationship in our OCCA case study.

A new threat

Once that groundwork was done, we looked ahead. Advances in AI, for good and for ill, were leading to a number of websites and platforms presenting themselves as official John Lennox websites, or as suppliers of unique material that John had never seen, never commissioned, and which was clearly pirated.

Our job became protecting John's work and assets, so that when people want to meet John, read his books, watch his videos and follow his material, they get the real John Lennox and not an AI imitation.

Three ways we protect his name

  1. Search adverts, funded by the Google Ad Grant. We commissioned and developed a series of adverts so that people searching for John see his official material at the top of the page. This brings consistent clicks and traction, protects his name and work, and grows the number of email subscribers and engaged people hearing from John directly every month.
  2. Partner links and authority. We worked with other organisations and ministries that have collaborated with John to make sure his name and position appear prominently on their websites, and that the links back to John's site work and are relevant. This matters for backlinks and website authority: it shows a strong connection, and search engines favour it.
  3. Continuous improvement and monitoring. We keep improving adverts, online presence and social media, including John's videos on YouTube, making sure that the adverts and connections around them are genuine and not fake or AI-generated. OCCA's operations manager regularly reports deepfake websites and videos as part of this work.
When people want to meet John, read his books or watch his videos, they should get the real John Lennox.

Why it matters

The aim is simple: to protect John's work as much as possible, and to make sure it reaches as many people as it can, because it points to the gospel of Jesus Christ.
