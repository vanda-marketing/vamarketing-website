Our story

Nearly 20 years in digital marketing, now devoted to Christian charities

Why a commercial digital marketing background led us to focus on charities — and how that background still shapes the way we work.

Where we started

Our agency has been built on marketing knowledge — and specifically digital marketing — since 2008. For nearly 20 years, we've worked with cutting-edge technology creators, and since 2020 we've developed commercial digital marketing specifically around lead generation, product development, e-commerce and sales.

Becoming Christian, and a change of focus

After becoming Christian, my wife and I made a decision: to focus our efforts on supporting Christian charities. Specifically, that means working in the areas of education and development, and creating real value within the charity structure — products, services and educational strategies that appeal to donors and support the growth of the organisation.

This is particularly valuable for charities that have products and tools they're able to sell or transact, including events that need exposure. Whether it's a course, a resource, a physical product or a conference, our job is to make sure the right people find it.

How we approach the work

We've found three approaches work together far more effectively than any one alone:

  • Natural keywords and organic content, reaching engaged audiences who are already seeking information and knowledge in your area and want to find out more.
  • Very specific, targeted advertising — Google ads and other targeted ads — that attract people actively seeking a product, service, event or piece of education.
  • Applying for and managing grants, such as the Google Ad Grant of up to $10,000 a month, which let us target, attract and convert the people who matter most to your charity.

The skill, in our experience, lies less in the channels themselves and more in identifying and creating the specific keywords, doing the research properly, and building content that genuinely attracts the right people. That groundwork is essential — without it, even a generous ad budget attracts the wrong audience.

Why we still work commercially

Our work in the commercial sector continues alongside our charity work. We work with a number of membership organisations and commercial businesses, and that continuous push for improvement keeps our knowledge and understanding of the market and technology growing.

It's also, quite practically, part of how we're able to offer our rates to Christian charities at £36 per hour — our commercial work helps subsidise the time we give to the charity sector.
