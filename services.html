How we work

Three approaches that work best together

Organic content, targeted advertising and grant-funded campaigns each teach us something the others can't. In our experience, a combination of all three is the most effective way to reach the people who matter most to your charity.

Organic search

SEO & keyword content

The first way we help you reach the right people is through natural keywords and engaged audiences who are already seeking information and knowledge in your area, and want to find out more. This is where the real skill lies — identifying and creating the specific keywords, doing the research properly, and then building content that attracts the right people.

  1. Keyword research

    We find the specific, natural phrases your ideal audience is actually searching for — not just generic industry terms.

  2. Content built to attract

    We create content around your education, products, services and events, aimed squarely at the people already looking for them.

  3. Ongoing refinement

    We track what's ranking and adjust — SEO is a continuous discipline, not a one-off project.

    We identify exactly who's actively seeking your product, service, event or piece of education.

  2. Build targeted campaigns

    Very specific Google ads and targeted ads, aimed at that audience rather than a broad, generic one.

  3. Convert the visit

    Landing pages built to turn that attention into an enquiry, a booking, a donation or a sale.

Paid search

Targeted advertising

The second way we help you reach the right people is by attracting those actively seeking whether it's a product, a service, an event or a piece of education — through very specific Google ads and targeted advertising, rather than broad campaigns aimed at anyone who might be interested.

Google Ad Grants

Grant application & management

The third way is by applying for and managing grants, such as those given by organisations like Google, worth up to $10,000 (around £8,000) a month. That budget lets us target, attract and convert the most valuable people your charity needs to be talking to — without it coming out of your own advertising budget.
