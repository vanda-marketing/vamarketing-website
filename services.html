How we work

Services built around long-term relationships

Organic content, targeted advertising and grant-funded campaigns each teach us something the others can't, and a membership and community model ties them together, so supporters become advocates. We work with charities over the long term, because that is how the combination works best.

Organic search

SEO & keyword content

    We find the specific, natural phrases your ideal audience is actually searching for — not just generic industry terms.

  2. Content built to attract

    We create content around your education, products, services and events, aimed squarely at the people already looking for them.

How we work

A clear process, built around your goals

Our marketing strategy is a little different. Our average working period with charities is 12 to 24 months, because the work is built on understanding your goals and the people you want to reach, then making content that genuinely connects with them.

1

Strategy

We build a content strategy rooted in your goals and in the psychology of the people you want to connect with, backed by keyword research and search trends that show where they are already looking.

2

Content plan

From the strategy we plan the articles, podcasts and videos you need to reach the people and the searches we have identified.

3

Deployment

Using that research alongside AI tools such as Gemini and ChatGPT, we shape content for the areas you want to target. You can create it yourselves, or our expert team can write it for you at competitive rates.

4

Optimise and publish

Before anything goes live, we optimise it across every channel so the right sequence of information and human interaction leads to the highest level of engagement.

Our goal is never content for its own sake. It is new subscribers, donors, members and event attendees, whichever you most want to focus on.
